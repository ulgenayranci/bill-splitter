<objective>
Add a scan-time guardrail that validates and reconciles OCR figures BEFORE users claim items.

Root cause (verified): `item.priceCents` is treated as the FULL LINE TOTAL by all downstream math, but OCR returns the PER-UNIT price into `priceCents` with `quantity` separate, and nothing multiplies. "Tuborg ×2 @ 450" is split as 450 instead of 900. The fix concentrates at INGESTION + a new pure reconciliation layer + DISPLAY. If reconciliation stores the true LINE TOTAL into `priceCents`, all existing billMath works unchanged.

Purpose: Stop systematic undercharging on multi-quantity lines and surface possible misread/missing items before money is split.
Output: Pure `reconcileScannedBill()` (fully tested), expanded OCR contract (unit price + line total + subtotal), `Item.unitPriceCents`, scan-flow reconciliation + guardrail summary, and multi-qty card display showing unit + line total.
</objective>
